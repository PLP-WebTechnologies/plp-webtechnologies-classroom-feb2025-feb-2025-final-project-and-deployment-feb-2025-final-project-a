Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this tracking number to monitor your package's journey on the carrier's website or in your account on our website.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items in new, unworn condition with original tags and packaging. Some items, like personalized products or intimate apparel, may not be eligible for return. Please visit our Shipping & Returns page for more details.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary depending on the destination. Please note that any customs fees, import taxes, or duties are the responsibility of the recipient.

How do I care for my LUXE products?

Each LUXE product comes with specific care instructions tailored to its materials and construction. Generally, we recommend gentle care for all our products to maintain their quality and extend their lifespan. If you've misplaced your care instructions, please contact our customer service team for guidance.
